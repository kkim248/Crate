Crate Design System

A calm, tactile system for a read-only library of unfinished music projects. Light grey cloud and wisteria grey with deep indigo as the only strong colour. This page is the human-readable mirror of docs/design.md, which is the source of truth.

Mobile

Below 900px the same system becomes a phone layer, following Apple Music, Spotify and Deezer: a large title, a bottom tab bar, and a mini player above it. Filters, project details, the details popup and menus open as bottom sheets with a grabber. Listen is one scrolling screen with a large ring and four equal actions. Recap is one column. Tap targets are 40px or larger, inputs are 16px so iOS does not zoom, hover is never required, and safe-area insets keep controls clear of the notch and home indicator.

Do's and Don'ts

Do

  • Say it in plain words: "No audio", "audio file".
  • Keep indigo as the only action colour.
  • Offer Undo after any action that hides or marks a project.
  • Omit values the data cannot supply.
  • Keep every control reachable by keyboard.

Don't

  • Use the word "bounce" in the interface.
  • Add generated artwork or fake data.
  • Use pure black or white, glows or gradient text.
  • Put text on neumorphic surfaces.
  • Write to, move or delete anything in the source folders.
